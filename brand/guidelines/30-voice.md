# Voice and copy

ConnectIn talks like a senior consultant who has done this a thousand times: calm, specific, on the founder's side.

## Principles

- **Start from the founder's question.** "Thinking of starting a business in February?" works because it is what they are already asking.
- **Concrete beats clever.** "4–6 weeks for a complete, stress-free closure" is the best line on the page: a number and a promise. Write more like it.
- **Name the real cost, then the fix.** "Improper liquidation can freeze your accounts" is fair. Don't escalate to fear ("haunt you") — it undercuts the calm.
- **One idea per post.** If the hero needs two pointers, it's two posts.

## Headline formula

`lead-in (soft, italic)` + `KEYWORD (the topic)` + `tag (where / for whom)`

- *Ready to* **LAUNCH** · in Dubai
- *The truth about* **BUSINESS SETUP** · in Dubai
- *7* **COSTLY MISTAKES** · when you start
- *Closing your* **BUSINESS** · the right way

## Do / don't

| Do | Don't |
| --- | --- |
| "Here's what you need sorted" | "Here's What You Need Sorted" (title case) |
| "Skip the mistakes. Start right." | "Build it Right from Day 1" (mixed casing) |
| "Corporate tax is 9% above AED 375,000 profit. Here's who pays." | "Be TAX-SMART in Dubai's NEW Business Era" (vague, shouty) |
| Numbers written as numerals: 7, 4–6 weeks, AED 375,000 | "Seven", "a few weeks" |
| "DM us 'SETUP'" — one call to action | Three CTAs across the slide |

## Mechanics

- British/UAE spelling (licence, organisation), matching government sources.
- AED before the amount: AED 12,500.
- No emoji on the canvas. In captions, at most one, at the end.
- Hashtags in the caption only, 5–8, specific (#DubaiBusinessSetup, #UAECorporateTax), never on the image.
