# Post templates

Three layouts cover everything the feed publishes. All share the 1080 x 1350 canvas, the `margin` (96px), the `header-band` and the `footer-band`.

## Grid

- 6 columns, 24px gutters, 96px outer margins → column width 124px.
- Headline stack spans columns 1–5 (it never touches the right margin, so it never fights the carousel icon or the image).
- Baseline: 8px (`space-1`). Every vertical gap is a spacing token.

## 1. Hero — Daylight (cover of an advisory carousel)

- Header band: logo top-left.
- Headline stack at y = 272: lead-in → keyword → tag, `space-2` between lead-in and keyword, `space-4` before the tag.
- Pointer (▸ + 1–2 lines) below the stack, `space-8` gap, columns 1–3.
- Image zone: bottom-right, subject cut out on `surface-mist`, may bleed off the bottom edge.
- Footer: website left, swipe cue right.

Use for: "Ready to LAUNCH", "The TRUTH about BUSINESS SETUP", "What kind of DOCUMENTS".

## 2. Carousel slide — Daylight

- Header band: `label` counter (STEP 03 OF 07) top-left in `gold`, logo moves to the last slide only.
- `numeral` or `title` for the point, then `body` (max 40 words), then an optional fact chip on `surface-deep`.
- No imagery, or one small icon. The slides are for reading.
- Footer: swipe cue right; on the last slide, replace it with the call to action ("DM us 'SETUP'").

## 3. Premium — Navy

- Ground `surface` (navy theme). Gold keyword, `ink` lead-in.
- Centre alignment allowed. Numerals and gold do the work; no photo required.
- Use for: announcements, new regulations (Corporate Tax, e-invoicing), seasonal greetings, milestones, the closing slide of every carousel.

## Grid rhythm

Plan the profile 3 posts at a time (one row): two Daylight, one Navy. That gives the page a steady dark beat without going gloomy.

## Carousel structure

1. Hero (hook) 2. The problem in one line 3–6. One point per slide 7. Navy closing slide: what ConnectIn does + one call to action.
