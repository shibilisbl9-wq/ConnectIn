For shoots and AI prompts
The image
RECIPE
LIGHT
High-key, cool morning light from the left. Soft shadow under the subject, nowhere else.
GROUND
Seamless mist #EAF1F6 fading to white. No other gradients, no busy backdrops.
SUBJECT
One per post: your team, real documents, real Dubai. Bleeds off the bottom or right edge.
MATERIALS
Navy wool, brushed brass, white marble, paper, glass. Gold is metal, never glitter.
NEVER
Red, petals, targets, mannequin crowds, cracked letters, ghost words, text inside images.
PROMPT BASE
High-key studio photograph, pale blue-grey seamless backdrop (#EAF1F6), soft morning window light from the left with faint blind shadows, one subject, navy and brushed-brass accents, empty space top-left for a headline, no text, no logos.
